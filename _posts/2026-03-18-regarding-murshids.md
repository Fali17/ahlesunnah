The Murshid (Shaykh) is the ladder to the Heavens;

How else does the arrow fly, if not from the Bow?

-By Jalaluddin Rumi

"The Shaykh is the ladder to Heaven...Meaning: The Shaykh is the ladder and the means of ascension. The seeker is like an arrow, and the Shaykh is like the bow. Just as the arrow only flies high and hits the mark when released by the strength of the bow, the seeker only ascends through the spiritual "Himma" (resolve/power) of his Shaykh. If you enter under the will of a Perfect Shaykh, you will reach a station that a thousand years of unguided worship could never attain."

(Sharh Mathnawi)
