**Sunan al-Tirmidhi, Hadith 3235**

Muhammad ibn Bashshar narrated to us, saying: Mu'adh ibn Hani, Abu Hani al-Yashkuri, narrated to us, saying: Jahdam ibn Abd Allah narrated to us, from Yahya ibn Abi Kathir, from Zayd ibn Sallam, from Abu Sallam, from Abd al-Rahman ibn A'ish al-Hadrami, who narrated to him from Malik ibn Yakhamir al-Saksaki, from Mu'adh ibn Jabal, who said:

**One morning, the Messenger of Allah, ﷺ, was delayed in coming to us for the dawn prayer, until we could almost see the sun's disc. He then came out quickly, and the iqamah was called for the prayer. The Messenger of Allah,ﷺ, prayed, keeping his prayer brief.**

When he had finished with the salutation, he called out to us in a loud voice and said to us:

**“Remain in your rows as you are.”**

He then turned towards us and said:

**“Indeed, I shall tell you what kept me from coming to you this morning.
During the night, I got up, performed ablution, and prayed as much as had been decreed for me. I became drowsy during my prayer and grew heavy with sleep. Then, suddenly, I found my Lord, Blessed and Exalted, in the most beautiful form.**

He said, **‘O Muhammad!’** (ﷺ)
I replied, **‘At Your service, my Lord!’**
He said, **‘Over what are the Highest Assembly disputing?’**
I replied, **‘I do not know, my Lord.’**
He repeated this three times.
He then placed His palm between my shoulders, until I felt the coolness of His fingertips between my breasts. Then everything was unveiled to me, and I knew **(fa-tajallā lī kullu shayʾ wa-ʿaraftu)**.
He said, **‘O Muhammad!’**
I replied, **‘At Your service, my Lord!’**
He said, **‘Over what are the Highest Assembly disputing?’**
I replied, **‘Concerning the expiations.’** He asked, **‘What are they?’** I replied, **‘Walking to attend congregational prayers, sitting in the mosques after the prayers, and completing ablution thoroughly under trying conditions.’**
He asked, **‘And what else?’**
I replied, **‘Feeding people, speaking gently, and praying at night while people are asleep.’**
He said, **‘Ask.’**
I replied:
**‘O Allah, I ask You for the doing of good deeds, the abandoning of wrong deeds, love for the poor, and that You forgive me and have mercy upon me. And when You intend a trial for a people, take me in death without my being tried by it. And I ask You for love of You, love of those who love You, and love of a deed that brings me nearer to Your love.’**

The Messenger of Allah, ﷺ, said:
**”‘Indeed, it is true; so study it, and then learn it.’”**

Al-Tirmidhi then states:
This is a hasan sahih hadith.
I asked Muhammad ibn Isma'il [al-Bukhari] about this hadith, and he said:
“This is a hasan sahih hadith. This is more authentic than the hadith of al-Walid ibn Muslim, from Abd al-Rahman ibn Yazid ibn Jabir, who said: Khalid ibn al-Lajlaj narrated to us, saying: Abd al-Rahman ibn A'ish al-Hadrami narrated to me, saying, **‘I heard the Messenger of Allah, ﷺ,’” and he then mentioned the hadith.
This version is not preserved in this form. In his narration, al-Walid stated that Abd al-Rahman ibn A'ish said, “I heard the Messenger of Allah, ﷺ.”
However, Bishr ibn Bakr narrated this hadith from Abd al-Rahman ibn Yazid ibn Jabir with this chain of transmission, from Abd al-Rahman ibn A'ish, from the Prophet, may Allah's peace and blessings be upon him. This is more authentic.
Abd al-Rahman ibn A'ish did not hear directly from the Prophet, ﷺ.
