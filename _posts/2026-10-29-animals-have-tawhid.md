title: "Animals,etc have tawhid"

**Qur’an 17:44** says: **“There is nothing that does not glorify Him with praise, but you do not understand their glorification.”**

**“The meaning of glorification (tasbih) is declaring Allah transcendent above imperfection, obedience, submission to His Lordship, and their being indicative of His existence and His oneness (tawhid).**

**‘And there is no thing except that it glorifies Him with His praise.’**

Ibn Abbas said: **‘There is no thing except that it is living.’**

Al-Hasan and al-Dahhak said: **‘He means everything that possesses a spirit.’**

Qatadah said: **‘He means animals and plants.’”**


Reference: **Al-Tha'labi, Al-Kashf wa al-Bayan 'an Tafsir al-Qur'an (The Unveiling and Clarification in the Exegesis of the Qur'an), vol. 6, p. 102, commentary on Surah al-Isra (17:44).**

There is also a report from Ibn Masʿūd: **“We used to hear the food glorifying while it was being eaten.”**
