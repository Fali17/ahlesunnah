A Flawed Yet Fascinating Caliphate Pattern 

Ibn al-Jawzi:

"Abu Bakr al-Suli mentioned that people say: Every sixth person who assumes authority over the people since the beginning of Islam must inevitably be deposed. I considered this and found it remarkable."

 **(Al-Muntazam, 17/332, 17/333)**


Al-Dahabi criticized this method by saying:

"I say: This passage is flawed in several respects."

"Deposition is therefore not restricted to every sixth person, even if the counting were correct."

But still its interesting though:
